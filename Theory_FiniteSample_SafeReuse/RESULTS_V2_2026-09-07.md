# Results v2 — certifiability trilemma

Date: 2026-09-07

Status: exploratory theory branch only; frozen manuscript and U6–U11 evidence remain unchanged.

## Research question

Can adaptive reuse of completed historical performance evidence be made finite-sample safe against current-only evaluation without paying away the information gain that reuse is supposed to provide?

## v2 answer

Not by a borrowing cap alone, and not cheaply by a naive honest split.

The branch now supports a sharper impossibility/possibility theory:

1. **Same-audit magnitude-only protection fails in the strongest bounded sense tested.** A single bounded construction works for every `0<omega<=1`: every fixed convex weight toward history is beneficial, but a same-audit rule using only weights `0` and `omega` is harmful.

2. **Role separation restores finite-sample certifiability.** An independent decision audit plus a valid confidence set yields a high-probability conditional MSE no-harm certificate relative to the evaluation-only direct estimator.

3. **Fair full-budget comparison produces an exact information boundary.** In an iid sample-mean problem, full-budget-safe role-separated convex reuse exists iff the historical bias is no larger than one standard error of the decision audit.

4. **The maximally tolerant weight has a direct interpretation.** The weight `w=m_s/M` replaces exactly the decision-audit fraction of the full direct estimator with historical evidence. It is the weight that maximizes the historical mismatch that can still be MSE-non-inferior to full-budget direct evaluation.

5. **High-confidence certification of that safe region is intrinsically weak in the Gaussian benchmark.** At a 5% false-certification level, even perfect historical agreement can be certified with probability at most about 8.23%.

This converts the earlier empirical statement `strict splitting is expensive` into an analytic **certifiability trilemma**.

## U10 connection

The frozen U10 result remains post-completion evidence, not prospective validation of the new theory.

Two U10 observations are relevant:

- all 8/8 observed mean shared-adaptive weights were below the oracle role-separated cap, yet 5/8 adaptive states were harmful while all 8/8 matched fixed-weight comparators were beneficial;
- the simple 95% exact-confidence-set split certificate had zero trigger probability in the U10-like iid-binomial probe.

The new theory explains both patterns without changing their evidential status.

For an iid Bernoulli benchmark at the frozen U10 accuracies, the one-standard-error boundary gives an oracle maximum decision-audit size of approximately:

- Georgia: `130.91` outcomes;
- CPSC 2018: `56.44` outcomes.

Above these decision-sample sizes, even exact knowledge of the current truth would leave no role-separated convex weight able to beat full-budget direct evaluation in that iid benchmark.

## What failed

The original hope was a practically useful 95% non-oracle safe-reuse rule based on a conventional independent decision/evaluation split.

That route failed as an operational method in the tested U10-like regimes. This failure should not be hidden or patched by adding more datasets.

The useful scientific result is the boundary revealed by the failure:

> current outcomes used to certify historical compatibility are not free; under fair same-total-budget comparison, the information spent protecting against same-audit dependence shrinks the safely tolerable historical mismatch, and high-confidence certification of that narrow region can itself have very low power.

## Novelty status after targeted search

Close prior work occupies the individual ingredients:

- finite-sample shrinkage risk improvement for biased/unbiased estimators;
- adaptive historical borrowing;
- finite-sample adaptive PPI failure and sample splitting;
- exact Type-I control after selective external borrowing;
- adaptive minimax response to unknown misspecification;
- bias-tolerance caps for dynamic borrowing.

The targeted search did not locate a direct prior result combining:

- universal fixed-weight benefit with harmful same-audit adaptation under every positive cap;
- a finite-sample post-deployment performance-reuse certificate;
- a fair full-budget role-separation feasibility boundary;
- the one-decision-standard-error limit;
- and a high-confidence certification-power ceiling.

Priority language must remain narrow: this is **not** the first finite-sample theory of adaptive borrowing.

## Manuscript decision

The branch has now crossed the earlier **Route-B scientific threshold**: there is a real analytical boundary rather than another explanatory diagnostic.

However, the manuscript should not yet be edited automatically. The correct next action is a short integration design exercise:

- determine whether the entire v2 result can replace/strengthen the existing PRESERVE theory without adding a fourth conceptual stage;
- keep the main-text mathematics minimal (likely one impossibility statement plus one certifiability boundary);
- place the Gaussian power ceiling, full proofs, and U10 iid diagnostics in SI;
- verify the MATLAB scripts locally before any numerical claim is copied into the manuscript.

If integration remains conceptually clean, the novelty can be upgraded from

`fixed-use evidence value can be lost under adaptive reuse`

to

`adaptive historical evidence has a finite-sample certifiability boundary: same-audit magnitude control is insufficient, while creating independent evidence for certification imposes an information cost that can make safe full-budget reuse statistically uncertifiable.`

That would be an identity-level upgrade of PRESERVE, not another dataset or metric increment.
