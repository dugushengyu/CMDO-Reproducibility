# Official data pages for CMDO U8

Use only CDC/NCHS public-use files. The MATLAB pipeline constructs the direct XPT URLs and downloads them automatically. These landing pages are provided for manual verification and recovery.

- [NHANES demographics files](https://wwwn.cdc.gov/nchs/nhanes/search/datapage.aspx?Component=Demographics)
- [NHANES examination files (`BMX` body measures)](https://wwwn.cdc.gov/nchs/nhanes/search/datapage.aspx?Component=Examination)
- [NHANES questionnaire files (`BPQ`, `SMQ`)](https://wwwn.cdc.gov/nchs/nhanes/search/datapage.aspx?Component=Questionnaire)
- [NHANES laboratory files (`GHB` glycohaemoglobin)](https://wwwn.cdc.gov/nchs/nhanes/search/datapage.aspx?Component=Laboratory)
- [August 2021–August 2023 laboratory page](https://wwwn.cdc.gov/nchs/nhanes/search/datapage.aspx?Component=Laboratory&Cycle=2021-2023)

Before the PREPARE seal is reviewed, do not download or inspect:

- `GHB_I.XPT` (2015–2016 reserve outcome)
- `GHB_J.XPT` (2017–2018 reserve outcome)
- `GHB_L.XPT` (2021–2023 reserve outcome)
