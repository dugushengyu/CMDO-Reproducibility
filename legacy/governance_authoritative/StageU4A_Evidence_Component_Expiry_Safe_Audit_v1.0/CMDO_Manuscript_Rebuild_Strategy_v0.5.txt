ONE-SENTENCE CLAIM

Deployed AI performance is generally not identifiable from unlabeled target data, becomes progressively observable through direct evidence, and is governed by shared error components whose target-specific mixtures create finite evidence-validity horizons; a reliability-gated sequential audit can exploit indirect evidence early and withdraw it before it becomes harmful.

PAPER LOGIC

Performance is latent after deployment.
Source competence and unlabeled target shift do not uniquely identify target performance.
T3-A supplies the locked-blind falsification of transport-only prediction.

ACT II — EVIDENCE MAKES PERFORMANCE OBSERVABLE
U0–U2 establish finite-budget scaling, label efficiency, medical non-null behavior and metric-dependent geometry.
Performance observability is an evidence state rather than a fixed model property.

ACT III — THE SIMPLE UNIVERSALITY ACCOUNT FAILS
U3A proposes hard candidate classes.
U3B freezes their prospective predictions.

ACT IV — THE DEEPER THEORY
The transferable basis is a set of error components:
persistent floor, regular first-order variation, and higher-order finite-budget variation.
Observed exponents are local slopes generated by target-specific mixtures.
Transport evidence has a finite validity horizon whenever residual bias persists.

ACT V — SAFE METHOD
Develop a reliability-gated, budget-adaptive sequential audit with:
direct uncertainty;
transport reliability;
support and disagreement gates;
automatic evidence withdrawal;
no-harm fallback;
stop/sample/abstain actions.

ACT VI — NEW PROSPECTIVE TEST
A new untouched multi-family reserve must predict:
late-budget trajectories;
evidence-validity horizons;
safe adaptive performance;
decision efficiency.
